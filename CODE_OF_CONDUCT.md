# Community conduct

Mino welcomes contributions and questions in Chinese or English, from people of any background or experience level.

Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Assume good faith, explain disagreements with evidence, and respect a maintainer's decision about the scope of this small project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized attention, repeated personal attacks, spam, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Do not post credentials or private screenshots.

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces explicitly maintained for Mino. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or block accounts when necessary, proportionate to the behavior. Maintainers are subject to the same standards.

Report a concern through a private maintainer channel if one is already available. Otherwise, open an issue requesting a private contact without identifying affected people or sharing sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities should follow SECURITY.md.

This is a community policy, not a promise of a particular response time or dispute-resolution service.
